SWW v0.9.13 scope refinement

Requested fixes:
- Scope reticle not centered on the vanilla crosshair.
- Visible ghosting/doubling still distracting.

Applied changes:
- Simplified the overlay to a clean cyan ring and guide marks.
- Removed the custom inner white/red center reticle entirely.
- The vanilla crosshair now serves as the exact center aiming point.
- Increased font ascent to lift the overlay upward toward screen center.
- No gray shadow is baked into the asset.

Note:
- Minecraft title rendering can still add its own subtle text shadow. This version minimizes that effect by simplifying the center geometry.

Preserved:
- DC-17 unchanged.
- DC-15S unchanged.
- DC-15A damage/rate/zoom unchanged.
- v0.9.6 audio unchanged.
- Blaster bolt unchanged.
- Lightsaber behavior/audio unchanged.
